New here? How CASCADE works

What it does

  • Answers questions about plant equipment from approved documents (one-point lessons, interlock tables, datasheets) and maintenance work orders.
  • Shows how repeated failures link into failure chains, and how long it took before a lesson was shared.
  • Attaches an evidence tag to every statement so you can open the exact source.

What it cannot do

  • It has no live plant data. It cannot tell you the current state of equipment or whether a start condition is met right now.
  • It is not a safety authority. It never approves bypassing, changing or overriding a safeguard; those requests are refused and escalated to Management of Change (MOC).
  • It only knows the committee sample dataset. Approved documents are indexed for two pilot assets.

How to read an answer

  1. Status first: the icon and label say whether CASCADE answered, needs more detail, did not answer, or refused and escalated.
  2. Then the plain answer and its sections. Each line ends with evidence tags such as E1 · OPL.
  3. Select a tag to see the source and the cited line. Anything under “Not verified by CASCADE” must be confirmed in the field.
